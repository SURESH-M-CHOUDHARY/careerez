# CareerEz – Your Personal AI Career Guide

**CareerEz** is an AI-powered platform designed to help students and job seekers navigate their careers with clarity and confidence. It offers intelligent tools for skill assessment, resume/CV generation, mock interviews, and real-time job insights — all in one place.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dashboard**  
  Get insights into trending jobs, skill market demand, and personalized recommendations.

- **AI-Powered Resume Builder**  
  Instantly generate ATS-optimized resumes tailored to your industry and skills.

- **CV Generator**  
  Create academic or project-based CVs for specific purposes.

- **Mock Quiz Engine**  
  Take MCQ quizzes generated by AI, customized to selected job titles or technologies.

- **Voice-Based Mock Interviews**  
  Experience real-time, AI-generated mock interviews with speech-to-text evaluation and feedback.

- **Job Alerts**  
  Receive curated job recommendations via background task automation.
